The Developer Behind the Screen

Hi, I'm Patryk Dudka! I am an indie-minded Unity Game Developer with 5+ years of experience. I specialize in turning high-concept gameplay ideas into robust, optimized systems that players love interacting with.

My workflow focuses heavily on clean architecture, sound design integrations, and cross-platform publishing pipeline management. Whether building custom localization structures, designing deep gamepad behaviors, or squeezing performance out of interface layouts on Steam Deck, I strive to merge technical excellence with handcrafted creative flair.

I thrive in the gray area between logic and art—designing flexible C# engines while maintaining a love for weird, retro, and experimental games.
